MODULE 4 — DECISION ANALYTICS ENGINE

Package: acs.analytics

1. DecisionAnalyticsEngine

- evaluateDecisions(): Displays basic decision-evaluation messages.
- generateReport(...): Creates an analytics report using incident counts, response-time data, and vehicle utilization.
- compareStrategies(...): Creates a strategy comparison object.
- compareTwoStrategies(...): Compares two strategies using average response time and incidents resolved. Returns the winning strategy name or "Tie".

2. AnalyticsReport
   Stores total incidents, resolved incidents, average response time, and vehicle utilization percentage.

3. IncidentAnalytics
   Stores total, pending, resolved, and cancelled incident counts and calculates active incidents.

4. ResponseTimeMetrics
   Stores response-time statistics and calculates average response time.

5. StrategyComparison
   Stores a strategy's name, average response time, resolved incidents, and vehicle utilization.

6. VehicleUtilization
   Stores vehicle activity time and available time and calculates utilization percentage.
